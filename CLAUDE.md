# Claude repository entrypoint

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) first. It is the canonical operating manual for all
coding assistants, including Claude.

Then read [teammate setup](docs/TEAM_SETUP.md) and
[AI teammate handoff](docs/AI_TEAMMATE_HANDOFF.md). Inspect `.ai/state.yaml`, the
assigned task/approval, `.ai/checkpoint.yaml` and relevant reviews before editing.
Do not treat a proposed roadmap or another agent's handoff as owner approval.
Do not create a separate Claude task ledger or duplicate architectural rules here.
