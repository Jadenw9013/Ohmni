## Remaining open items

- Pins absent from schematic need explicit NC/physical binding treatment, not a guessed ground.
- Leakage, CMC values, parasitic capacitance, surge working limits and full CMR/return-loss model are unresolved.
- Bound reference (D069): Bourns PT61017PEL, an explicit alternative to the Pulse H1102NL representative (same 16-terminal 1.27 mm-pitch SMD form and TD/RD/TX/RX pin functions, 1CT:1CT, 350 uH min OCL). H1102NL itself stays unbindable: its line-side choke resistance and pins 4, 5, 12 and 13 are unpublished.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the explicitly bound reference (2026-10-11, D069): Working-voltage and complete physical pin binding remain unestablished; no isolation-safety acceptance. Resolution: the Bourns schematic and DCR table bind every functional pin; pins 4, 5, 12 and 13 have no function in the schematic and are left out of the terminal map, so the compiler refuses any connection to them. Working voltage is not published (1500 VAC, 1 mA, 2 s PRI-SEC is a hipot test); under D066 the recipe's first limitation states no isolation or safety acceptance.
- Centre-tap half-winding resistance (blocks any centre-tap-biased design), function of pins 4/5/12/13, working voltage, leakage, common-mode choke, capacitance and return-loss model remain unsourced. The physical numbering direction of pins 2-16 follows the package convention; the Bourns drawing marks only pin 1.

## Conflicts

- 350 uH OCL is now sourced for H1102NL itself; previous reliance on another family is unnecessary.
- D069: H1102NL specifies DCR per half winding (0.65 ohm) while PT61017PEL specifies it per full winding (0.6 ohm, pins 1-3); the values are not interchangeable. Bourns body 12.80 x 9.30 x 5.65 mm max and land rows 10.50 mm vs the 3D H1102NL default 12.70 x 9.53 x 6.80 and 10.16 mm.
- D069: the class rwinding 0.5 ohm and CMC_RDC 0.1 ohm (ASSUMPTION) are supported by neither sheet; the bound part's sourced values are used.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference (former blocker: Working-voltage and complete physical pin binding remain unestablished; no isolation-safety acceptance. Resolved 2026-10-11, D069; mains/isolation/safety acceptance is excluded by the recipe's first limitation).
