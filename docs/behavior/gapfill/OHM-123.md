## Remaining open items

- Firmware and USB protocol are not simulable in ngspice.
- Tj,thermal model and current grouping require a newer primary revision.
- DC values are preliminary design targets according to p379footnote5.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the bound reference (2026-10-11, D075): Physical body mismatch and unresolved rating-critical DC footnotes prevent automatic runtime binding. Resolution: The 'body mismatch' is not real: Atmel-7766J p2 gives a 44-lead TQFP 10x10 mm body and the spec's TQFP-44 is SOT376-1 10x10x1.0 mm (12 mm is the lead-tip span). The DC footnote is resolved by binding the preliminary DC values with a plain limitation that Microchip calls them preliminary design targets (p385, owner decision 2026-10-11); the absolute maximum ratings (p383) are not preliminary. The part is bound as its electrical pins in the factory reset state (JTAG enabled); firmware is not modeled.
- Every DC value used (leakage 1 uA, RRST 30-60 kohm, RPU 20-50 kohm) is a preliminary design target (Table 29-1 note 5); characterized silicon values are not published.
- No thermal resistance or junction-temperature limit is published; junction temperature stays unknown.
- USB PHY, the USB pad regulator (UVCC/UCAP) and VBUS sensing are not modeled.
- The affine LVTTL input thresholds cannot be expressed by the pad's VCC fractions; the din/und nodes keep the asset defaults and are not read by any check.

## Conflicts

- p378 drive tests use10mA@5V/5mA@3V,while p379footnotes say20/10mA and mention portsA/G absent from the pin map. Retain conflict; do not manufacture aggregate limits.
- Source TQFP body10x10mm differs from library TQFP44 body12x12mm.
- D075: the 'Source TQFP body 10x10 mm differs from library TQFP44 body 12x12 mm' conflict is withdrawn: the spec member is SOT376-1 10x10x1.0 mm; 12 mm is the lead-tip span.
- D075: Table 29-1 footnotes 3/4 cite 20 mA/10 mA test conditions and port groups A/G that the 32U4 does not bond out; the aggregate group limits remain recorded only, not enforced.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference (former blocker: Physical body mismatch and unresolved rating-critical DC footnotes prevent automatic runtime binding. Resolved 2026-10-11, D075).
