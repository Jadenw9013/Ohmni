## Remaining open items

- Firmware and USB protocol are not simulable in ngspice.
- Tj,thermal model and current grouping require a newer primary revision.
- DC values are preliminary design targets according to p379footnote5.

## Conflicts

- p378 drive tests use10mA@5V/5mA@3V,while p379footnotes say20/10mA and mention portsA/G absent from the pin map. Retain conflict; do not manufacture aggregate limits.
- Source TQFP body10x10mm differs from library TQFP44 body12x12mm.

## Simulation blockers

- Physical body mismatch and unresolved rating-critical DC footnotes prevent automatic runtime binding.
