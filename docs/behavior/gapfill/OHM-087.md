## Remaining open items

- Per-segment thermal coupling, aggregate common-lead current and exact fit across current/temperature remain open.
- Multiplex duty does not permit exceeding the specified peak-current pulse conditions.
- Primary PD/IF table does not explicitly allocate the ratings per segment or as a package total. No such allocation is asserted.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the bound reference Broadcom HDSP-5503 (2026-10-11, D070): Per-segment and common-lead aggregate power/current allocation is not established; do not use table PD/IF as a rated complete-display envelope. Resolution: Kingbright's current SC56-11EWA sheet still lists PD/IF/VR without saying per segment or per package. Broadcom's 14.2 mm (0.56 in) family sheet AV02-1107EN (2024) titles every rating row 'per Segment or DP' (75 mW, 30 mA DC derated per Figure 8, 90 mA peak at 10% / 1 kHz, VR 3.0 V test-only), and HDSP-5503 (single digit, common cathode, right-hand decimal) has the same pin map as the archived SC56-11EWA. The binding moves to HDSP-5503; the per-segment allocation closes from the source, and the common lead is only a derived sum (no common rating is published, stated as a limitation).
- No package-total power, Tj or thermal resistance is published for HDSP-55xx; multi-segment self-heating acceptance stays unknown.
- Derating above the 55 C knee is not encoded; peak and multiplexed operation are not checked.
- The common-anode HDSP-5501 is not bound.

## Conflicts

- The primary source gives 75 mW per die; do not use the older 105 mW assumption.
- D070: the class pd_seg 105 mW (labelled S14/SC56-11EWA) disagrees with the archived SC56-11EWA table (75 mW) and with HDSP-5503 (75 mW per segment); 105 mW is not used.
- D070: Kingbright VR 5 V and peak 160 mA (1/10, 0.1 ms) vs Broadcom VR 3.0 V (test only) and peak 90 mA (10%, 1 kHz); the binding uses Broadcom values only.
- D070: Kingbright VF 1.9/2.3 V at 10 mA vs Broadcom HDSP-55xx red 2.05/2.5 V at 20 mA.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference Broadcom HDSP-5503 (former blocker: Per-segment and common-lead aggregate power/current allocation is not established; do not use table PD/IF as a rated complete-display envelope. Resolved 2026-10-11, D070).
