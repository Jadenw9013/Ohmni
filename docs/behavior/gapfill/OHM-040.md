## Remaining open items

- Exact temperature/voltage derating implementation, charging limits and balancing network require reference binding.
- Model ESR must name its time/frequency condition; one universal ESR is not supported.
- Surge voltage and failure timing remain unsourced for this exact reference.

## Conflicts

- The behavior default is 10 F / 2.5 V while the AVX part matching the 10 x 20 mm 3D body is 5 F. The same diameter can hold 10 F at 30 mm height (SCCS30B106SRB), which is a different body.
- Do not silently combine Eaton HB electrical ratings with the AVX mechanical reference.
- The generic 20 mohm ESR is below all three listed maxima for the matching AVX reference.

## Simulation blockers

- Unresolved electrical/reference/geometry binding and condition-specific current limits; stored energy and short-circuit heating are safety relevant.
