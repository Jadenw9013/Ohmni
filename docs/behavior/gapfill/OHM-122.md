## Remaining open items

- Firmware/peripherals cannot be simulated in ngspice; any later model must be interface-only.
- Junction thermal parameters,pin capacitance and dynamic pad model remain unresolved.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the bound reference (2026-10-11, D075): No validated interface-only recipe; firmware/protocol behavior is not simulable. Resolution: Microchip DS40002061B (Microchip-hosted, archived) gives the reset state (port pins tri-stated, p85), the factory fuses that make PB6/PB7 port pins and PC6 the RESET input with its pull-up (p37, p13, p292), every absolute maximum (p308), the leakage, pull-up and threshold values (Table 30-1, p322-323) and the pin map (p12). The part is bound as its electrical pins in that reset state (one hashed dio.lib pad per port pin, RESET without the upper clamp); firmware is not modeled.
- No thermal resistance or junction-temperature limit is published for the ATmega328P in DS40002061B or 8161D; junction temperature stays unknown.
- Core supply current versus clock is not modeled (Active 8 MHz 5 V: 5.2 typ / 9 max mA, Table 29-8, recorded only).
- Output drive is reachable only through a future explicit pin-state declaration; the reset-state binding never drives a pin.
- Clamp injection limits are not published; only the per-pin 40 mA and VCC/GND 200 mA stress ratings are checked.

## Conflicts

- The retained research combines explicitly named2009 electrical and2012 summary revisions; exact modern ordering-revision reconciliation remains open.
- Spec package Tj150C/theta50C/W is not sourced for this MCU and cannot become a rating.
- D075: the spec (7.2/7.4/7.5) gives ATmega328P VOL <= 0.4 V at IOL 20 mA, VCC 5 V and fits rol = 20 ohm; DS40002061B Table 30-1 (p322) states VOL <= 0.9 V there (TA = 85 C row; 1.0 V at 105 C). The binding records 0.9 V (rol = 45 ohm); the hashed dio.lib B1 bench still validates the 0.4 V point, which is not this part's value.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference (former blocker: No validated interface-only recipe; firmware/protocol behavior is not simulable. Resolved 2026-10-11, D075).
