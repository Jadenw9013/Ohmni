## Remaining open items

- The series page quotes 2500 or 4000 VAC isolation without assigning the exact AS-103 option; do not invent a working voltage.
- Primary through-hole routing direction, burden protection and open-secondary hazard require explicit circuit binding.

## Conflicts

- AS-103 is 1:300, 200 mH minimum, 10 ohm maximum and 50 mA secondary; the earlier 1:200 / 10 mH / 3 ohm / 75 mA defaults are incorrect for this reference.
- A three-pin illustrated center-tapped part cannot silently represent non-center-tapped AS-103.

## Simulation blockers

- Physical/reference and primary-conductor binding conflict; open-secondary operation is hazardous.
