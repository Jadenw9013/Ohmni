## Remaining open items

- ESR at frequency/temperature, ESL and failure timing are still model assumptions.
- The sleeve is not a guaranteed electrical insulation system.
- ESR versus frequency/temperature and ESL remain unsourced (tan d at 120 Hz and |Z| at 100 kHz are maxima only).
- Rated ripple at temperatures other than 105 C; reverse-voltage tolerance (none permitted).
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the explicitly bound reference (2026-10-11, D067): Rating-critical part voltage and ripple/lifetime limits need an exact series binding. Resolution: Nichicon UPW series catalog (archived) gives, for the exact part UPW1E221MPD: 25 V, 220 uF, tan d 0.14, leakage 165 uA, impedance 0.117 ohm (20 C/100 kHz), rated ripple 555 mA rms (105 C/100 kHz) with frequency multipliers, and endurance 3000 h at 105 C with rated ripple for phi 8. Voltage, ripple and lifetime are therefore bound to an exact series row.

## Conflicts

- Spec default ESR 0.15 ohm and ESL 15 nH are ASSUMPTION; the part gives tan d 0.14 at 120 Hz (bound 0.844 ohm) and |Z| <= 0.117 ohm at 100 kHz; the card models no ESR (D067).
- Spec RLK default 1e8/220 = 4.5e5 ohm vs sourced 25 V / 165 uA = 1.52e5 ohm; the binding uses the sourced value (D067).
- Spec 3D default member 10 x 16 mm vs bound part 8 x 11.5 mm.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference Nichicon UPW1E221MPD (former blocker resolved 2026-10-11, D067).
