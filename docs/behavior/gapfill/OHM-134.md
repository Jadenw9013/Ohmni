## Remaining open items

- ExternalL/C,feedback,loopbehavior and switch-node waveform require explicit circuit validation.
- DGK VSSOP thermal184.3K/W is not this DSG package.
- Bound to TI's own TPS62160 PSpice switching model (2026-10-11, D074); it needs PSpice compatibility mode and a 20 ns internal step, so a 300 us start-up costs about 2.5 min of ngspice CPU.
- Thermal, temperature drift and Tj are not modeled by the TI deck; ratings come from datasheet facts.
- The runtime probe uses an ideal 2.2 uH test inductor (the datasheet's standard value) with the bound Murata 22 uF capacitor (OHM-026); the bound Bourns 1.8 uH inductor (OHM-045) stops ngspice at TI's first switching edges (nonlinear-inductance card).

## Conflicts

- D074: the class BUCKCORE (inductor inside, VOS regulated to an internal VSET) cannot be bound to the real SW/FB/VOS/PG pinout; TI's own model is bound instead.

## Simulation blockers

None newly identified; other stated limitations remain.
