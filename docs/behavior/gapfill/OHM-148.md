## Remaining open items

- No opened rating sheet establishes drive ceiling,terminal voltage,current or ESR for this exact part. (D073: superseded for terminal voltage: Murata JGC40-5039 rates D.C. 6 V max, 15 Vp-p input amplitude and 100 V DC withstand for 5 s; Murata rates voltage, not drive power.)
- MotionalC1/C0 assumptions and numeric package-pin assignment remain unresolved; characterization diagram gives circuit roles only. (D073: the numeric terminal map is now sourced: JGC40-5039 p3 '(1) Input, (2) GND, (3) Output'; (1)/(3) are interchangeable with a +/-0.60% initial tolerance. Motional C1/C0 remain unsourced.)
- Motional C1 and C0 (or L1/Qm) for CSTLS8M00G53 from a Murata document naming the part; the P17E manual's 8 MHz comparison column names no part and is a DigiKey-hosted mirror, so it cannot bind.
- Library lead (1)/(3) vs X orientation is not tied to the Murata marked-face view (electrically interchangeable).

## Conflicts

- Characterized-40..85C MCU combination does not override consumer-series-20..80C specification.
- D073: class S13 cites a 2020-04-01 chipdip copy of the CSTLS8M00G53-B0 sheet; the archived Murata spec JGC40-5039 is dated 2017-03-27. The shared values (8 MHz, +/-0.5%, +/-0.2%, 25 ohm, 15 pF) agree.
- D073: the spec's 'no drive-level or voltage rating found' is superseded: DC 6 V, AC 15 Vp-p and withstand 100 V/5 s are rated.

## Simulation blockers

- Missing rating-critical drive limit and primary terminal map; no sourced runtime binding.
