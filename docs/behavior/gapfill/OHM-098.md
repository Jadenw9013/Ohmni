## Remaining open items

- Digitized SOA and transient thermal constraints remain required for power-stress claims.
- Neither threshold voltage nor a 10 V RDSon establishes suitability for 3.3 V gate drive.
- Regulator/BJT/isolated-TO220 variants are separate functional bindings.
- Single-pulse table L0.47 mH differs from note2 L0.48 mH; retain that source discrepancy. Avalanche operation stays unsupported until a sourced pulse/SOA binding replaces the destructive-value guard.

## Conflicts

- IRFZ44N94 W/0.63 W-per-C and RthJC1.5 imply different thermal limits; apply the lower allowable power from both, never raise94 W to100 W.
- Datasheet 2026 revision dates are now observed directly in primary PDF bytes.
- The class eas=0.53 J uses the typical destructive value as an operating ceiling. The primary source explicitly says this is outside rated limits. Its calculated thermal ceiling is150 mJ; do not use the old0.53 J acceptance expression.

## Simulation blockers

None newly identified; other stated limitations remain.
