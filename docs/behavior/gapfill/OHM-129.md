## Remaining open items

- Protocol conversion is not simulable in ngspice.
- Reference-specific Tj/thetaJA and optional pad thermal consequences remain unresolved.

## Conflicts

- The primary diagram says GND(Optional) for the exposed pad; the generic mandatory-ground assumption is not adopted.
- The5.8V input limit is conditional onVIO>2.2V and applies only to named pins.

## Simulation blockers

- Missing thermal evidence and no validated interface recipe.
