## Remaining open items

- Supply current and per-channel current remain unsourced assumptions; refuse a rated L1 load model.
- Pixel protocol/color decoding belongs to L3, outside ngspice.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the bound reference Worldsemi WS2812B-V7 (2026-10-11, D070): Rated supply-current model is blocked until an exact-version current specification is sourced. Resolution: Worldsemi's own site publishes the WS2812B-V7 (5050) sheet (V1.3). Its 'VF constant current curve' plateaus at 16.12 mA per output from 0.6 V to 6.0 V output voltage, LED characteristics are specified at 16 mA, and the quiescent current is <= 1 uA (no signal, RGB off), at VDD=5V. The L1 supply load is bound to WS2812B-V7 as a full-white static load iq + 3 x 16.12 mA. The active control-IC current while clocked is unpublished and stays a stated limitation; the protocol stays L3 (D066 item 3).
- The control IC's active supply current while receiving data is unpublished.
- The VDD headroom each color needs for regulation is unpublished; the class 3.0 V / 0.5 V dropout knee stays an authored assumption.
- No power-dissipation or thermal limit is published for V7.
- The single-wire protocol, gray levels, DOUT drive and reset timing are L3 and not simulated; the record stays not simulable (spec layer L3).

## Conflicts

- Source absolute maximum ends at 5.3 V while electrical test heading extends to 5.5 V; use the conservative intersection for any exact-reference checks.
- The 5.0 mm body and 5.4 mm lead span are distinct dimensions in this drawing; do not silently change existing provisional geometry.
- The notch identifies pin 3, not pin 1, for this revision.
- D070: class parameters ich 20 mA per channel and iq 1 mA (ASSUMPTION) vs V7 16.12 mA typical per channel and <= 1 uA quiescent (off).
- D070: the recorded absolute_supply_range [3.5, 5.3] V is from the older Adafruit-hosted WS2812B revision; V7 lists +3.3..+5.3 V. The binding uses V7 only.
- D070: class t_reset 50 us (older revision) vs V7 RES > 280 us (L3 only, not used).

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference Worldsemi WS2812B-V7 (former blocker: Rated supply-current model is blocked until an exact-version current specification is sourced. Resolved 2026-10-11, D070).
