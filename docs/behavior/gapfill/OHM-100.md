## Remaining open items

- SOA/transient thermal curves and exact cropped-middle-lead binding remain to validate.
- Source status remains research_required; opening a datasheet alone does not complete the model.
- D072: Infineon publishes the exact IRLR/U8721PbF model (irlr_u8721pbf.spi, archived); the D059 note that no IRLR8721 model card is published was wrong. Binding it needs two owner decisions: (a) netlist.validate_mosfet_cards requires IS=0 while the vendor card sets IS=1e-32; (b) the MOSFET runtime probe compares against the maximum Rds(on) +/- 3 %, whereas a typical vendor model gives 6.27 mohm against 8.4 mohm max, so a typical-vs-maximum probe mode is needed.
- Vendor model threshold (2.59 V at 25 uA) is above the datasheet VGS(th) range 1.35-2.35 V; its thermal network is not wired.

## Conflicts

- IRLR8721 power is65 W atTC25 C and33 W atTC100 C; the earlier33 W-at25 conflict was a table-condition extraction error.
- The IRLR3410 gate limit is +/-16 V, not a generic +/-20 V.
- IRLR8721 headline65 A is calculated; the explicit50 A package limit further caps current. RDS(on) pulse-footnote linkage is ambiguous between page2 note4 and page12 pulse note3.
- D072: STATE/D059 said 'Infineon publishes no IRLR8721 model card'; Infineon's design resources list irlr_u8721pbf.spi (archived).
- D072: the spec's 'RthJC 2.3 K/W with PD 33 W (150/2.3 = 65 W)' is not a conflict: PD is 65 W at TC = 25 C and 33 W at TC = 100 C (p1).
- D072: vendor model threshold 2.59 V exceeds the datasheet VGS(th) maximum 2.35 V.
