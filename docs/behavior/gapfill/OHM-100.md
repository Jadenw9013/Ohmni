## Remaining open items

- SOA/transient thermal curves and exact cropped-middle-lead binding remain to validate.
- Source status remains research_required; opening a datasheet alone does not complete the model.
- Bound to Infineon's own irlr_u8721pbf SPICE model (2026-10-11, D074); the D059 note 'Infineon publishes no IRLR8721 model card' was wrong: Infineon's design-resources listing names irlr_u8721pbf.spi.
- The vendor model's threshold is about 2.59 V (VTO=2.5898; 2.592 V at 25 uA in ngspice), above the datasheet VGS(th) maximum 2.35 V: near threshold it conducts less than any datasheet part.
- The cropped middle lead (2) is drain like the tab (4), so cropping it changes no electrical role; the tab carries D.
- SOA and transient thermal are not modeled; the vendor thermal subcircuit irlr_u8721pbft is in the asset but not wired.

## Conflicts

- IRLR8721 power is65 W atTC25 C and33 W atTC100 C; the earlier33 W-at25 conflict was a table-condition extraction error.
- The IRLR3410 gate limit is +/-16 V, not a generic +/-20 V.
- IRLR8721 headline65 A is calculated; the explicit50 A package limit further caps current. RDS(on) pulse-footnote linkage is ambiguous between page2 note4 and page12 pulse note3.
- D074: D059 'Infineon publishes no IRLR8721 model card' is wrong; Infineon's IRLR8721 design resources list irlr_u8721pbf.spi.
- D074: vendor model threshold 2.59 V vs datasheet VGS(th) 1.35 .. 2.35 V (model above the maximum).
