## Remaining open items

- Polarity-to-footprint permutation, thermal derating and inrush/arc limits remain unverified.
- Bound reference (D069): Amass XT60PW-M30.G.Y per the 2025V0 product specification (7.20 mm pitch, 2 x 2.90 mm power holes); the legacy V1.2 values are not used.
- Former simulation blocker closed only in the D066 sense (2026-10-11, D069): Safety-critical variant, polarity and thermal scope unresolved. The variant is closed (M30, 2025V0). Polarity and thermal scope are not: the 2025V0 drawing numbers no pin and prints no + / -; the catalogue side view shows one '+' on a housing face that nothing ties to a PCB hole. The recipe is a symmetric contact-resistance model with polarity explicitly unassigned; polarity must be checked on the physical part.

## Conflicts

- Legacy 45 A/500 V/1000 uses vs catalogue 35 A/500 V withstand/100 uses vs M30 page 35 A/80 V working/1.2 milliohm. Retain the lower original 30 A current assumption, 80 V working ceiling and 1.2 milliohm conservative resistance for review only; do not enable a model.
- No 60 A continuous claim; PCB variant is not the 12 AWG cable connector. Battery short-circuit and hot-mating arc hazards remain.
- D069: the earlier review note proposed a 30 A current; the sourced XT60PW-M30 2025V0 rating is 35 A (delta T <= 85 K), with 80 V DC and <= 1.2 milliohm. The bound part's 2025V0 values win for this binding; the class default 30 A / 60 A / 0.55 milliohm (cable XT60, S3) does not apply.
- D069: 3D spec power hole 'dia 2.7' vs 2.90 mm in 2025V0; the '0.6 x 1.7' feature is drawn in 2025V0 as 0.80 x 1.90 mm locating slots 13.50 mm apart.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference (former blocker: Safety-critical variant, polarity and thermal scope unresolved. Closed only in the D066 sense, 2026-10-11, D069; the unclosed safety part is the recipe's first limitation).
