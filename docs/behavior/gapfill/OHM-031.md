## Remaining open items

- Exact series/orderable part and its ripple-current, lifetime, voltage and temperature ratings remain unbound.
- ESR at frequency/temperature, ESL and failure timing are still model assumptions.
- The sleeve is not a guaranteed electrical insulation system.

## Conflicts

- The default three-pin snap-in variant cannot tie an auxiliary terminal to minus without an exact-part pin diagram; general manufacturer guidance requires isolation.

## Simulation blockers

- Rating-critical part voltage and ripple/lifetime limits need an exact series binding.
- Three-pin auxiliary-terminal connectivity unresolved; only the separately mapped two-pin variant may proceed after rating binding.
