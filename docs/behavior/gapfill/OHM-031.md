## Remaining open items

- ESR at frequency/temperature, ESL and failure timing are still model assumptions.
- The sleeve is not a guaranteed electrical insulation system.
- Three-pin (auxiliary-terminal) variant: no pin diagram or function archived; it remains unresolved and must not use the two-pin card.
- ESR versus frequency and ESL remain unsourced.
- Life under ripple and a 105 C rating (LKG is an 85 C series).
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the explicitly bound reference (2026-10-11, D067): Rating-critical part voltage and ripple/lifetime limits need an exact series binding. Three-pin auxiliary-terminal connectivity unresolved; only the separately mapped two-pin variant may proceed after rating binding. Resolution: Nichicon LKG catalog (archived) row LKG1V222MESZAK: 2200 uF, 35 V, tan d 0.30, rated ripple 1.45 A rms at 85 C/120 Hz, endurance 1000 h at 85 C, leakage rule 3√CV after 5 min; its snap-in drawing shows exactly two terminals at 10 mm pitch with ⊖ at the polarity bar. This binds voltage, ripple and life for the two-pin variant. The three-pin variant remains blocked (no 3-pin drawing archived; Nichicon guideline requires auxiliary terminals isolated, conflicting with the spec's tie-to-minus rule). Alternative 105 C general-purpose choice if preferred: Nichicon LGU1V332MELZ (3300 uF 35 V 22 x 25, 1450 mA at 105 C/120 Hz, leakage 1.01 mA listed, 3000 h at 105 C with ripple), also archived.

## Conflicts

- The default three-pin snap-in variant cannot tie an auxiliary terminal to minus without an exact-part pin diagram; general manufacturer guidance requires isolation.
- Spec default ESR 0.03 ohm (ASSUMPTION) vs the tan d bound 0.181 ohm at 120 Hz; the card models no ESR (D067).
- Spec RLK default 1e8/2200 = 4.5e4 ohm vs sourced 35 V / 832.466 uA = 4.20e4 ohm; the binding uses the sourced value (D067).
- Spec 3D default 25 x 40 mm vs bound 22 x 20 mm; LKG is an 85 C series.
- Spec three-pin rule 'tie the extra pin to -' conflicts with the Nichicon guideline (auxiliary terminals isolated); unchanged, three-pin stays unresolved.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference Nichicon LKG1V222MESZAK (former blocker resolved 2026-10-11, D067).
