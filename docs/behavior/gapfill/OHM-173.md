## Remaining open items

- No unambiguous +/- pin labeling in the archived drawing; library terminal1 at -X and footprint pad1 at +X require verified polarity/permutation.
- No thermal derating, PCB copper condition or peak-current duration.
- Bound reference (D069): Amass XT30PW-M30.G.Y per the 2025V0 product specification (5.00 mm pitch, 2 x 1.80 mm power holes); the legacy V1.2 values are not used.
- Former simulation blocker closed only in the D066 sense (2026-10-11, D069): Safety-critical polarity and thermal derating unresolved. Neither half is closed by evidence: Amass numbers no pin and prints no + / - mark, and publishes no derating beyond 20 A at 16 AWG with delta T <= 85 K. The recipe is a symmetric contact-resistance model with polarity explicitly unassigned; polarity must be checked on the physical part.
- Polarity (+ / -) to pad mapping, thermal derating versus ambient/PCB copper, peak/inrush/arc limits, and whether 1.2 milliohm is per contact or per mated pair remain unsourced.

## Conflicts

- PCB wire field literally says PCB; do not apply a cable AWG rating.
- Battery short circuits, reversed polarity and hot-plug arcing remain hazards; 30 A is not a continuous current rating.
- D069: recorded V1.2 values (15 A rated, 30 A instantaneous, 0.8 milliohm, 500 V DC, 1000 uses) differ from the 2025V0 XT30PW-M30 specification (20 A at 16 AWG, delta T <= 85 K; <= 1.2 milliohm; 80 V DC; 100 cycles). The bound part's 2025V0 values win for this binding; the spec is unchanged.
- D069: the product page says plug life 20 times; the 2025V0 PDF says 100 times. 3D spec power hole 1.7 mm (V1.2) vs 1.80 mm in 2025V0; peg holes 1.0 vs 1.10 mm.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference (former blocker: Safety-critical polarity and thermal derating unresolved. Closed only in the D066 sense, 2026-10-11, D069; the unclosed safety part is the recipe's first limitation).
