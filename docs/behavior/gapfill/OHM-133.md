## Remaining open items

- External feedback/COUT,stability,noise and NR timing require circuit-level validation.
- Current-limit1.1..2A is a trip characteristic,not safe continuous capacity.
- Bound to TI's own TPS7A8001 PSpice model (2026-10-11, D074). Its usage notes say input/quiescent current, temperature effects, noise and the NR function are not modeled (NR is 10 Mohm to GND).
- The model's dropout is a fixed 0.35 V and its current limit 1.4 A; the datasheet dropout is 0.25/0.35/0.5 V maximum at 0.5/0.75/1 A and the limit 1.1..2.0 A.
- Stability against COUT and its ESR is not validated by the macro-model; the 4.7 uF minimum stays a design requirement.

## Conflicts

- Chosen3x3TPS7A80 is not the6x5W25Q flash alternative.
- D074: the spec's 'reuse BEH-REG-LINEAR' (a three-terminal fixed LDO_R6) cannot represent FB/EN/NR; TI's own model is bound instead.

None newly identified; other stated limitations remain.
