## Remaining open items

- Package-terminal ordering differs from a simple geometric CCW assumption; bind explicitly.
- Leakage, common-mode saturation and fitted HF loss remain unresolved.

## Conflicts

## Simulation blockers

- Mains-rated winding binding and missing saturation/thermal model need explicit handling; insulation test voltage is not a working voltage.
