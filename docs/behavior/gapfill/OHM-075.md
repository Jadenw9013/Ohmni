## Remaining open items

- The sheet does not give RthJA or Tj maximum. The copied 350 K/W is not established; thermal acceptance must be refused.
- This is an orange reference, not a rating for every color or multi-die 10 mm LED.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the bound reference Kingbright WP813ID (2026-10-11, D070): Junction-temperature/thermal acceptance cannot use the unsourced 350 K/W placeholder. Resolution: Kingbright's current-format sheet for the same 10 mm lamp family with the GaAsP/GaP die, WP813ID (DSAE9418 V.10A, 2024), tabulates Tj 125 C, RthJA 500 C/W and RthJS 290 C/W (FR4, pad >= 16 mm2 per pad) with PD/IF/IFP/VR and its own 25 C forward curve. The binding moves from the orange WP813ED (no thermal data; the current kingbrightusa and LCSC copies are byte-identical) to the red WP813ID, and the 350 K/W placeholder is not used.
- Forward-current derating versus ambient (p3 curve) is not encoded; ratings are TA=25 C values.
- Colors other than WP813ID red, and the earlier WP813ED orange reference, are not bound.
- Physical 3D flange and lead geometry stays the existing provisional model.

## Conflicts

- The sheet shows a 2 mm flange thickness, whereas the 3D record has a provisional placeholder; geometry remains unchanged.
- D070: the recorded 350 K/W RthJA (copied from 5 mm lamps) disagrees with WP813ID's sourced 500 C/W; the placeholder would underestimate junction heating by about 30%.
- D070: spec default if_max 20 mA (ASSUMPTION) vs the sourced 30 mA DC for WP813ID (and WP813ED).

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference Kingbright WP813ID (former blocker: Junction-temperature/thermal acceptance cannot use the unsourced 350 K/W placeholder. Resolved 2026-10-11, D070).
