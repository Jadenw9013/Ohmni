## Remaining open items

- Mains safety also requires application/clearance/protection design; a class model cannot certify safety.
- ESL and long-term failure timing remain uncertain.
- Bound reference (D069): WIMA MKX2AW31004C00 (MKP-X2, 0.1 uF, 305 VAC 50/60 Hz, PCM 15, W 6 x H 12.5 x L 18 mm, pdf p3); tolerance, packing and pin-length suffixes unselected.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the explicitly bound reference (2026-10-11, D069): Unbound mains safety part; no claim of safe mains operation. Resolution: the part is now bound to one exact orderable row; the safety half is not closed by evidence and is not claimed: under the owner's D066 decision it is the recipe's first limitation (no mains, isolation or safety acceptance; no discharge path, creepage/clearance, fusing or approval check).
- No AC-rating (305 V rms) or pulse dV/dt rule is executed for this binding; the BEH-CAP-FILM rating branch needs a DC derating fact this X2 part does not publish, so its rating rows stay unknown.

## Conflicts

- New manufacturer revision rates this series 305 VAC; retain the conservative generic 275 VAC default until a specific revision and part are bound.
- D069: the bound MKX2AW31004C00 is rated 305 VAC (rev 03.26); the class BEH-CAP-FILM default 275 VAC / 388.9 V peak (older revision, S20) does not apply to this binding and its VRPK flag is dropped. The bound part's sourced value wins for this binding; the spec is unchanged.
- 3D spec OHM-038 default body W 8 x H 14 x L 18 at PCM 15 is not an orderable 0.1 uF MKP-X2 row (B00 5 x 11 x 18, C00 6 x 12.5 x 18); C00 is bound. WIMA p2 gives lead d = 0.8 for PCM >= 15 against the 3D default 0.6.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference (former blocker: Unbound mains safety part; no claim of safe mains operation. Resolved 2026-10-11, D069; mains/isolation/safety acceptance is excluded by the recipe's first limitation).
