## Remaining open items

- Firmware and peripheral protocols are not simulated.
- GPIO alternate-function/type mapping and power-sequence rules remain application-specific.
- Board thermal path and programmed I/O state require explicit circuit context.
- CIO 5pF is typical, not a worst-case maximum; no maximum can be inferred from guaranteed-by-design footnote.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the bound reference (2026-10-11, D075): No validated interface-only recipe; firmware execution is not simulable. Resolution: ST DS8626 Rev 12 (ST-hosted, archived) gives the reset state (all I/Os floating inputs during and after reset unless bracketed or noted, p48), the five-volt-tolerant structure of every pin (Table 7), the absolute maxima including the FT input limit and injection limits (Tables 11-13, p80-81), the I/O static characteristics (Table 48, p116-117), the NRST pull-up (Table 51, p121) and thermal resistance (Table 98, p185). The part is bound as its electrical pins in that reset state; firmware is not modeled. The recorded LQFP64 pin map was wrong at pins 49-63 and is corrected from Figure 12 (p43), cross-checked against Table 7.
- The debug pins PA13, PA14, PA15, PB3 and PB4 come out of reset in their JTAG/SWD function; DS8626 does not state their pulls, so they are not modeled.
- PC13-PC15 depend on the backup domain after reset (Table 7 note 4) and are not modeled.
- Core, regulator and peripheral supply current is not modeled (Table 20 values recorded only).
- Output drive is reachable only through a future explicit pin-state declaration.

## Conflicts

- Selected STM32F405/407 reference is explicit; other MCUs cannot inherit these pin numbers or ratings.
- LQFP body height1.4mm is different from generic TQFP1.0mm for OHM-124/125; no automatic physical compatibility claim.
- D075: maximum_supply_current recorded as 150 (STM32F405RGT6 LQFP64; suffix6; sum of all supply lines; sepa) is superseded by 240 from the bound source.
- D075: the recorded LQFP64 pin map (from the 2012 Rev 3 Keil copy) was wrong at pins 49-63: it read 49 VSS, 50 PA14, 51 PA15, 52 PC10, 53 PC11, 54 PC12, 55 PD2, 56 PB3, 57 PB4, 58 PB5, 59 PB6, 60 PB7, 61 BOOT0, 62 PB8, 63 PB9. DS8626 Rev 12 Figure 12 (p43) and the Table 7 LQFP64 column give 49 PA14, 50 PA15, 51 PC10, 52 PC11, 53 PC12, 54 PD2, 55 PB3, 56 PB4, 57 PB5, 58 PB6, 59 PB7, 60 BOOT0, 61 PB8, 62 PB9, 63 VSS, 64 VDD; the binding uses the corrected map.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference (former blocker: No validated interface-only recipe; firmware execution is not simulable. Resolved 2026-10-11, D075).
