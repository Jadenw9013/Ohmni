## Remaining open items

- IS/N/RS, CJO and recovery fit remain approximations; fitted nominal parameters are not maximum ratings.
- Typical curve digitization, reverse leakage versus temperature and thermal transient remain unfinished.

## Conflicts

- 100 V is repetitive peak reverse voltage; continuous reverse rating is 75 V. They must not share one runtime limit.
- 150 mA average rating at VR=0 is distinct from 300 mA continuous-current entry; do not collapse pulse/average/DC conditions.

## Simulation blockers

None newly identified; other stated limitations remain.
