## Remaining open items

- Wi-Fi,Bluetooth,USB,firmware and flash transactions are not simulated.
- Typical drive40/28mA is not a safe absolute per-pin current rating.
- Junction thermal data and sustained per-pin current limits are unestablished.

## Conflicts

- The0.5A supply figure is a minimum capacity requirement,not a maximum-current rating.

## Simulation blockers

- No validated interface recipe and missing rating-critical thermal/per-pin limits.
