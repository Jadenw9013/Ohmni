## Remaining open items

- Actual end resistance and wiper resistance are not fixed by worst-case bounds.
- Maximum permissible wiper current is not established.
- A failure transient or wear model is not established.
- D072: the bound reference is the explicit alternate Bourns 3361P-1-103GLF (6.7 mm body, 0.5 W), not the 3314G spec default (4.5 mm, 0.25 W); a catalog part must match the 3361P footprint for the binding to apply.
- Maximum wiper current is published for neither 3314 nor 3361; the wiper stays unconnected and loaded-wiper simulation is refused.
- Wiper position, end resistance and contact-resistance variation are bounds only and are not modelled.
- 3314G itself remains unbound for pin functions (only its Z style names the wiper and CW/CCW pins).

## Conflicts

- The source does specify end resistance: max(1% of R, 2 ohm). The spec calls it unspecified and proposes a 2 ohm placeholder. Style R is limited to 50 V, not 300 V.
- D072: the record's 3314 v_max 300 V applies to styles G, J and H, while only the Z style names the wiper and CW/CCW pins; the 300 V cannot be paired with a 3314 style that has named pins. Bourns 3361 gives one schematic and one 300 V rating for all styles, so the binding uses 3361.

## Simulation blockers

None newly identified; other stated limitations remain.
