## Remaining open items

- Reactive parasitics remain unspecified; zero L/C is an idealized assumption, not measured package behavior.
- Thermal resistance derived from rating endpoints is not an independently measured board-specific value.
- Destructive-failure thresholds and timing are not characterized by the rating table.
- Z300 axial and 2 W variants are not covered by CPR05.
- A generic open-circuit failure at the continuous power limit is not a sourced fuse time/current curve.

## Conflicts
