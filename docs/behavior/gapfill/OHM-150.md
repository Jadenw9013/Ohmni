## Remaining open items

- The initial20mohm maximum is not a sourced10mohm typical;model choice must say upper-bound or assumption.
- Working voltage,actual mated variant and all-contacts-loaded temperature derating are not established by the series sheet.
- Numerical terminal order remains the library convention;the connector assigns no electrical polarity.
- The spec thermal resistance,bulk resistance,inductance and blanket0.7/0.8derating factors remain assumptions.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the explicitly bound reference (2026-10-11, D068): Rating-critical working voltage and full mating/thermal context unresolved. Resolution: Wurth 61300811121 publishes a continuous 'Working Voltage 250 V (AC)' (distinct from the 500 V AC 1-min withstand), 3 A rated current, 20 mOhm max contact resistance and -40..+105 C operating temperature that the same document defines as ambient plus self-rise, so the working-voltage and thermal-context blocker closes for this exact part. Mating context: modeled as numbered PCB pins P<k> to open mating contacts M<k> with Wurth's max contact resistance (same pattern as OHM-163/166). Harwin M20 (current reference) publishes no working voltage (prior round).
- Mating counterpart/test condition behind the 20 mOhm max is not stated by Wurth.
- No all-positions-loaded derating, Rth, bulk resistance or inductance sourced.
- Library footprint must match the Wurth hole pattern (2.54 mm, drill per datasheet) for the binding to apply.

## Conflicts

- This explicit Harwin reference has300gold operations,not Samtec1000or unrelated25-cycle defaults. Proof voltage cannot replace a continuous working-voltage rating.
- D068: Spec Rc default 10 mOhm (ASSUMPTION) vs Wurth 20 mOhm max: the binding uses 20 mOhm as an upper bound.
- D068: Existing entry facts (contact_current_max, contact_resistance_initial_max, operating_temperature_range) are Harwin M20 scoped; new Wurth facts use ref_* names to avoid silently re-scoping them.
- D068: Spec Vwork 250 V cites Samtec S1; Wurth independently gives 250 V AC working voltage for this part (agreement, now exact-part sourced).

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference Wurth Elektronik 61300811121 (former blocker: Rating-critical working voltage and full mating/thermal context unresolved. Resolved 2026-10-11, D068).
