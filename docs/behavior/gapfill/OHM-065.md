## Remaining open items

- Distinct PN, Schottky and TVS functions cannot share ratings. Exact current derating and thermal mounting remain required.

## Conflicts

- The spec assumed S2x current 2 A by scaling S1x. The actual Vishay S2M sheet rates 1.5 A at TL125 C; 1.5 A is the conservative reference ceiling.

None newly identified; other stated limitations remain.
