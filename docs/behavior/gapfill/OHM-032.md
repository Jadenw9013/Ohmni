## Remaining open items

- ESR at frequency/temperature, ESL and failure timing are still model assumptions.
- The sleeve is not a guaranteed electrical insulation system.
- ESL and ESR versus frequency/temperature beyond the 1.25 impedance ratio remain unsourced.
- Life under ripple and a part-specific rush-current limit.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the explicitly bound reference (2026-10-11, D067): Rating-critical part voltage and ripple/lifetime limits need an exact series binding. Resolution: Nichicon PCJ conductive-polymer catalog (archived) row PCJ1C101MCL4GS: 100 uF, 16 V, surge 18.4 V, tan d 0.12, leakage 320 uA (2 min), ESR 24 mohm (20 C/100 kHz), rated ripple 2700 mA rms (105 C/100 kHz), endurance 2000 h at 105 C. Voltage, ripple and life are bound to an exact row; ESR is sourced at 100 kHz so the tantalum-style AC probe applies.

## Conflicts

- Spec default ESR 20 mohm vs sourced 24 mohm maximum at 100 kHz; the binding uses 24 mohm (D067).
- Spec RLK 1e6 ohm vs sourced 16 V / 320 uA = 5e4 ohm (20x lower); the binding uses the sourced value (D067).
- Spec 'DER 0.8 (12.8 V operating)' is not stated in the PCJ datasheet (it gives surge 18.4 V); DER is not applied.
- Spec Style A outline 6.3 x 7.7 vs part 6.3 x 8 mm.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference Nichicon PCJ1C101MCL4GS (former blocker resolved 2026-10-11, D067).
