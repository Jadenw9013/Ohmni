## Remaining open items

- ESR at frequency/temperature, ESL and failure timing are still model assumptions.
- The sleeve is not a guaranteed electrical insulation system.
- ESR/impedance at high frequency and ESL remain unsourced.
- Life under ripple (the series states rated-voltage endurance only); vent clearance for 6.3 mm.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the explicitly bound reference (2026-10-11, D067): Rating-critical part voltage and ripple/lifetime limits need an exact series binding. Resolution: Nichicon UUX series catalog (archived) row UUX1E470MCL6GS: 47 uF, 25 V, 6.3 x 7.7, tan d 0.14, leakage 35.25 uA, rated ripple 78 mA rms (105 C/120 Hz) with multipliers, endurance 2000 h at 105 C. Exact value/voltage/case match to the spec default.

## Conflicts

- The vent-clearance table starts at 8 mm diameter and does not establish a clearance for the 6.3 mm default.
- Spec default ESR 0.8 ohm (ASSUMPTION) vs the tan d bound 3.95 ohm at 120 Hz; the card models no ESR (D067).
- Spec RLK default 1e8/47 = 2.1e6 ohm vs sourced 25 V / 35.25 uA = 7.09e5 ohm; the binding uses the sourced value (D067).

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference Nichicon UUX1E470MCL6GS (former blocker resolved 2026-10-11, D067).
