## Remaining open items

- Fit/SOA/transient thermal validation remains open; package-level status remains research_required.
- The reference specifies TO-247AC; AD and IGBT variants need separate physical/electrical bindings.
- D072: the spec's research_required reasons (pin order 'UNRESOLVED', 'no vendor model') are closed: the pin order G 1, D 2 and tab, S 3 is sourced from the archived IRFP260NPbF datasheet, and Infineon publishes irfp260n.spi (archived). The spec status cell is left for the owner to change to partial.
- The vendor card is not used by the binding: its threshold (4.36 V at 250 uA) exceeds the datasheet maximum 4.0 V, it is a typical-device model, and netlist.validate_mosfet_cards requires IS=0 while the card sets IS=1e-32. The authored IRFP260N_R6 recipe stays bound.

## Conflicts

- The source explicitly establishes gate1,drain2,source3; prior drain-gate-source extraction was wrong.
- D072: Infineon vendor model threshold 4.36 V at 250 uA exceeds the datasheet VGS(th) maximum 4.0 V.
- D072: the authored IRFP260N_R6 card uses VTO = 3.0 (ASSUMPTION); the vendor card has VTO = 4.363.
