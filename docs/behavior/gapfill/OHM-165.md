## Remaining open items

- Exact 24-pin drawing and series rating specification are blocked at manufacturer URLs; the archived brochure lacks a complete signal table, voltage rating and derating.
- The 16-contact catalog subset is not the full 24-contact reference footprint.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the explicitly bound reference (2026-10-11, D068): Missing exact contact rating and physical map; USB/PD protocols not simulable. Resolution: Default Amphenol 12401610E4#2A drawing (c12401610) remains HTTP 403. Explicit alternate GCT USB4110-GF-A (archived GCT drawing) gives a complete 16-contact pin/signal table, the footprint pad lettering, and per-group contact ratings (5.00 A VBUS collective, 6.25 A GND collective, 1.25 A A5/B5, 0.25 A per other pin), 48 V DC, 40 mOhm max initial. Contact rating and physical map close for this exact 16-contact USB 2.0 part; USB/PD protocol and CC logic stay out of scope (Rd/Rp are separate parts).
- 24-contact default (Amphenol 12401610) drawing still blocked
- Library footprint for USB4110
- USB protocol, enumeration and power negotiation are not simulated.
- Per-contact and collective current limits are now bound (D068): VBUS 5.00 A and GND 6.25 A collectively, CC 1.25 A, others 0.25 A per contact.

## Conflicts

- Family 5 A capability cannot be applied to a single VBUS contact or treated as USB-PD negotiation.
- D068: Spec OHM-165 states '1.25 A on B5' and that 'the A5/B5 rating asymmetry is real'; GCT USB4110 states '1.25A for A5/B5 pin' (both CC pins). Different part, but the spec's asymmetry claim should not be generalised.
- D068: Entry's family_extended_current_claim 5 A is Amphenol family; GCT's 5.00 A is collective over VBUS pins, not per contact.
- D068: The spec 3D model has 24 contacts; this binding is 16 contacts (catalog record HRO TYPE-C-31-M-12 is also 16).

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference GCT USB4110-GF-A (explicit alternate) (former blocker: Missing exact contact rating and physical map; USB/PD protocols not simulable. Resolved 2026-10-11, D068).
