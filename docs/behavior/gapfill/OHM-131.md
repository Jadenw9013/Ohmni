## Remaining open items

- Firmware and peripherals are not simulable.
- The read pinout does not establish the electrical connection of the central pad; do not copy a generic GND rule.
- Pin-specific five-volt tolerance,adjacent injection effects and all dynamic pad behavior remain to bind.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the bound reference (2026-10-11, D075): Exposed-pad electrical role and interface-only model unresolved. Resolution: The exposed-pad role is sourced: DS5319 Rev 20 p83 (UFQFPN48 outline note 3) recommends soldering the exposed pad to PCB ground, so the binding requires it on the VSS net with no conductive model. The interface-only model is the part's electrical pins in the reset state stated by RM0008 Rev 9 section 8.1.1 (Input Floating; JTAG pull-ups), with DS5319 Rev 20 limits; firmware is not modeled.
- PA14 (JTCK) has a reset pull-down the pad cannot express, and PB3 (JTDO) is a debug output; neither is modeled.
- PC13-PC15 (backup domain, Table 5 note 6) and pins 5/6 (OSC_IN/OSC_OUT after reset, note 7) are not modeled.
- RM0008 is the 2009 Rev 9 copy hosted by Arm Keil (st.com refused the archiver); a current ST-hosted copy needs an owner browser download.
- Core and peripheral supply current is not modeled.

## Conflicts

- D075: the EP open item 'do not copy a generic GND rule' is answered by the manufacturer for this package only (DS5319 Rev 20 p83); the VFQFPN36 note differs.
- D075: the recorded UFQFPN48 pin map (32mcu.com mirror) is unchanged in DS5319 Rev 20 Figure 9 for pins 1-48; the bound map adds the exposed pad as role EP (p83 outline note 3), which the binding requires on the VSS net.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference (former blocker: Exposed-pad electrical role and interface-only model unresolved. Resolved 2026-10-11, D075).
