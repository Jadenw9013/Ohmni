## Remaining open items

- MURS-type rectifier alternative lacks an exact selected MPN and remains unavailable; do not use SS34 ratings for it.
- Schottky leakage heating and mounting-dependent derating remain required.

## Conflicts

- The SMC thermal proxy 75 K/W is not the SS34 sheet value55 K/W; it may remain a conservative assumption but is not sourced to SS34.

None newly identified; other stated limitations remain.
