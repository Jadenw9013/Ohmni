## Remaining open items

- Backlight voltage/current and thermal rating are not given in this opened document.
- Controller instruction execution and text state belong to L3.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the bound reference Newhaven Display NHD-0216K1Z-FL-YBW (2026-10-11, D070): Do not simulate the assumed 100 mA/4.1 V backlight as a rated module load. Resolution: The archived WH1602B sheet states only 'Backlight Type LED'. Newhaven's own NHD-0216K1Z-FL-YBW specification gives the backlight as a supply load through an on-board resistor ('LED+ ... +5.0V via on-board resistor'): VLED 4.9/5.0/5.1 V and ILED 65/130/156 mA at VLED = 5.0 V, with VDD 4.5-5.5 V, IDD 0.5/1.0/3.0 mA at 5.0 V and the 16-pin map. The binding moves to this module, and the backlight is one resistance from the sourced 5.0 V / 130 mA point; the assumed 100 mA / 4.1 V diode is not used.
- Backlight I-V away from 5.0 V +/- 0.1 V and backlight thermal or derating data are unpublished.
- No absolute-maximum VDD is published in this sheet (only the 4.5-5.5 V operating range).
- Controller instruction execution, text state and timing are L3 and not simulated; the record simulates the supply and backlight pins only (D066 item 3).

## Conflicts

- Logic low is 0.6 V maximum, not a generic 0.3*VDD threshold.
- At25 C recommended VDD-VO is 3.6..3.8 V, not the prior 4.0 V assumption.
- D070: class backlight_if 100 mA / backlight_vf 4.1 V (ASSUMPTION) vs Newhaven 130 mA typical (65-156 mA) at 5.0 V through an on-board resistor.
- D070: class vlcd 3.2-5.2 V (S13) vs Newhaven VLCD 3.5-3.9 V and the archived WH1602B 3.6-3.8 V; class idd max 1.5 mA vs Newhaven 3.0 mA max.
- D070: the archived WH1602B values (IDD 1/1.2/1.5 mA, contrast 3.6-3.8 V, absolute VDD -0.3..7 V) belong to a different module and are not used.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference Newhaven Display NHD-0216K1Z-FL-YBW (former blocker: Do not simulate the assumed 100 mA/4.1 V backlight as a rated module load. Resolved 2026-10-11, D070).
