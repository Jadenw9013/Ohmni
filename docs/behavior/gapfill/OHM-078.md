- RthJA and Tj maximum are absent for the red reference; thermal acceptance must remain unknown.
- Green catalog role permutation remains explicit and unchanged; selecting a color does not establish a different MPN automatically.

## Conflicts

- The previously recorded 0.55mm versus source0.8mm package height mismatch remains.
- InGaN variants do not have a5V absolute reverse voltage listed in the maximum-ratings table; a leakage test at5V cannot establish it.

- Thermal acceptance lacks sourced junction/thermal parameters; generic color variants lack an exact reference.
