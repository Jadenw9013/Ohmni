## Remaining open items

- Exact pin mapping and current/temperature derating not archived; manufacturer product page and drawing returned HTTP 403.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the explicitly bound reference (2026-10-11, D068): Rating-critical scope and terminal binding unresolved; no USB protocol simulation. Resolution: Default Amphenol 10118194-0001LF drawing remains HTTP 403. Explicit alternate GCT USB3075-30-A (archived GCT drawing) labels 'Pin 1' and 'Pin 5' on both the recommended PCB layout and the front view, and states part-level ratings: 30 V AC, 1.0 A signal pins 2-4 / 1.8 A power pins 1,5, 30 mOhm max, -30..+85 C. Rating scope and terminal binding close for this exact part; USB protocol stays out of scope.
- Default Amphenol 10118194-0001LF drawing (browser download)
- Library footprint for USB3075 (pads differ from the Amphenol default)
- USB protocol, enumeration and power negotiation are not simulated.
- Per-contact current limits are now bound (D068): 1.8 A on pins 1/5, 1.0 A on pins 2-4.

## Conflicts

- Brochure gives 30 milliohm initial plus 10 milliohm change; presentation says 10 milliohm maximum. Retain 30 milliohm conservative initial resistance.
- Brochure defines 100 VAC as one-minute proof; presentation calls 100 VAC rated. Working-voltage scope remains unresolved.
- D068: Entry facts are Amphenol family brochure values (30 mOhm + 10 mOhm change, 100 VAC proof, 1 A presentation); not used.
- D068: Spec ratings cite Molex 105017 (1.8 A / 1 A, 30 V AC, -30..85 C); GCT USB3075 independently states the same pattern for this exact part.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference GCT USB3075-30-A (explicit alternate) (former blocker: Rating-critical scope and terminal binding unresolved; no USB protocol simulation. Resolved 2026-10-11, D068).

## Field evidence

## Primary-source sweep (2026-10-06)

- Corrected presentation_contact_resistance_max: was 10 milliohm, which is the allowed change after test, not the maximum. Amphenol's presentation and GS-12-641 give 30 milliohm initial and 40 milliohm after test.
- Primary added: Micro USB 2.0 Connectors (BASICSINOMICROUSB0523EA4 (record cites INOMICROUSB0215EA4)) https://cdn.amphenol-cs.com/media/wysiwyg/files/documentation/datasheet/inputoutput/io_usb_micro.pdf.
- presentation_current_rated: primary only partly supports it (Signal contacts 2,3,4: 1A (MIN) per single contact; power contacts 1,5: 1.8A; slide 4 specifications; GS-12-641 Rev C: 1.8 A for contacts 1 and 5 with 0.5 A on 2,3,4 simultaneously; product page lists 1.8 A).
- presentation_voltage_rated_ac: primary only partly supports it (Dielectric Withstanding Voltage: 100V AC for 1 minute; slide 4; it is a DWV proof test, not a working voltage (product page labels it 'Voltage Rating 100V AC')).
- Primary added: FCI Basics USB 2.0 Micro-B & AB Product Presentation (Feb 20th 2022) https://cdn.amphenol-cs.com/media/wysiwyg/files/documentation/customerpresentation/micro_usb_2_0_productpresentation.pdf.
