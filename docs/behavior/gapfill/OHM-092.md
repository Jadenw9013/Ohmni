## Remaining open items

- Exact OLED module MPN, connector pin order, onboard regulator and pull-ups are absent.
- Controller no-load current does not predict lit-panel/module current.
- I2C address/commands/pixel state are L3 requirements and must not be claimed as ngspice results.

## Conflicts

- The retrieved DC and I2C tables resolve controller-level missing data only; they do not verify 5 V module tolerance.

## Simulation blockers

- Unknown module terminal map and power/logic limits block module electrical simulation.
