## Remaining open items

- Exact dielectric, manufacturer part, voltage rating, loss and leakage remain unresolved.
- Generic disc construction does not establish a mains Y safety classification.
- A 100 nF disc value remains unbound: no primary 100 nF single-layer disc datasheet was found.
- Lead/ESL parasitics, bias and temperature curves remain unsourced.
- Y-class safety discs remain out of scope; this binding is not a safety part.
- Former simulation blocker resolved for the explicitly bound reference (2026-10-11, D067): Unbound generic disc voltage and safety class; no mains-use inference is permitted. Resolution: Vishay BCcomponents D series datasheet (doc 28549, rev 08-Jan-2026, archived) is a general-purpose DC single-layer disc family: 50 VDC rating, IR 10 Gohm min, Class 3 DF 5% max, and only EIA 198/IEC 60384-8/-9 approvals (no IEC 60384-14 X/Y safety rating). Binding D473Z39Z5VF6###R closes the voltage blocker and fixes safety_class = none from the source itself. Caveat: value is 47 nF, not 100 nF (the family max at 50 VDC); recorded as an explicit alternative (OHM-024/OHM-037 precedent). If the owner requires the 100 nF value, keep blocked: no primary 100 nF single-layer disc datasheet was found (Vishay D series, Vishay K series is multilayer, Murata/TDK 'radial' parts are leaded MLCCs).

## Conflicts

- Spec default 100 nF is above the Vishay D series 50 VDC maximum (47 nF Z5V, 22 nF Y5V); the binding is 47 nF (D067).
- Vishay p1 lists Z5V operating range -30 to +125 C but climatic category 30/85/21 (+85 C upper) for Class 3.
- Spec ESL 10 nH and ESR 0.1 ohm remain ASSUMPTION; spec RLK 1e10 matches the sourced 10 Gohm.

## Simulation blockers

- None for the bound reference Vishay BCcomponents D473Z39Z5VF6###R (former blocker resolved 2026-10-11, D067).
