## Conflicts

- S1M needs 85 K/W, not the 75 K/W S1A-S1J value used in the original generic power calculation.
- The 2024 S1M sheet gives Tj=175 C; retain the earlier 150 C conservative ceiling for an unspecified revision.
- SS14 hot reverse leakage is 6 mA in this 2020 table, not 5 mA (the SS15/SS16 column).
