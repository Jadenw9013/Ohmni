## Remaining open items

- No numbered terminal drawing independently binds the generic 3D numbering to this unmarked array.
- Document is an authentic-looking manufacturer datasheet hosted by a third party; primary-host relocation remains open.
- Failure timing and aggregate thermal coupling are not established.
- D072: Vishay no longer serves CRA06P (doc 31047) or CRA06E/S (doc 31002) - HTTP 404, ledger rows recorded; mirrors only. Bourns CAT/CAY16 shows the opposite-terminal topology but without terminal numbers. A numbered primary pin-to-element drawing, or an owner decision to bind CAY16-103J4LF from the unnumbered schematic, closes it.

## Conflicts

- The 5% reference grade has +/-200 ppm/K TCR; the spec assigns +/-100 ppm/K to a 5% default. +/-100 applies to 1% grade.

## Simulation blockers

- Confirm numbered pin-to-element map before simulating the physical array.
