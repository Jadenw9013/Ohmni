# Ohmni brand guide

## Identity

- **Product:** Ohmni
- **Tagline:** Build circuits. Understand why.
- **Category:** Evidence-first AI electronics engineering mentor

Ohmni helps people understand hardware ideas, turn them into engineering
requirements, ground decisions in datasheets, design and verify circuits, and
learn from the evidence. PCB generation is an important capability, not the
whole product identity.

## Personality

- Technically rigorous
- Curious
- Educational
- Transparent
- Approachable
- Builder-oriented

## Visual direction

Draw from electronics workbenches, oscilloscopes and engineering tools. Use
schematic and PCB visual language, clean technical typography, and clear
evidence/status indicators as a major UI motif.

Avoid futuristic AI clichés, excessive gradients, robot imagery, generic
circuit-brain logos, and claims of guaranteed correctness.
