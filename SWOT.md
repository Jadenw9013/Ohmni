# SWOT Analysis

## Strengths

- highly visual and tangible demo
- combines AI, electronics, simulation, EDA, and education
- strong hobbyist value proposition
- can demonstrate measurable verification
- open-source stack keeps costs low
- datasheet ingestion creates a strong niche-hardware story

## Weaknesses

- PCB correctness is difficult
- component universe is huge
- datasheets are inconsistent
- layout correctness is harder than schematic correctness
- physical validation may not fit hackathon timing
- live supplier APIs can be brittle

## Opportunities

- rising interest in AI-assisted EDA
- students and hobbyists remain underserved
- education can differentiate from automation-first tools
- bench instrumentation creates a long-term moat
- open-source community could contribute verified component knowledge

## Threats

- Flux
- CELUS
- Circuit Mind
- Quilter
- fast-moving open-source research
- users over-trusting AI-generated designs
- hardware liability
- supplier/catalog APIs changing
