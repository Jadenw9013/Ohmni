Describe something you want to build

What do you want to build?

Tell Ohmni what your device should do. It works out which parts you need, wires them together, checks its own work against the manufacturers' datasheets, fixes what it finds, and hands you files a factory can build from — explaining each decision as it goes.

  • You get a schematic, a circuit board, a parts list, and manufacturing files.
  • You do not need to know any electronics vocabulary. Ohmni explains as it goes.
  • Different from a chatbot: a chatbot describes a circuit. Ohmni builds one, then runs 24 deterministic checks plus KiCad's own independent checker over it, and shows you every result — including what it could not check.
